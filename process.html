From stock to stunning.

Five stages, in order, every time. Knowing what happens at each one is the easiest way to know what you're paying for.

01

Consult

We start with the car, not a catalogue: what you drive, how you use it, and the look you're actually after. You choose the finish, style and coverage — full wrap, colour change, graphics, or a fleet spec — and we tell you honestly what will and won't work on that vehicle.

02

Design

Before anyone touches your car, the idea becomes a clean visual plan — panel layout, colour breaks, graphic placement. You see and approve it before install day, so there are no surprises once the vinyl is cut.

03

Prep

This is where most wraps actually fail or succeed. A full decontamination wash, panel inspection and surface prep — any dirt, wax or damage left underneath will show up in the finish, so we don't skip it.

04

Wrap

Careful, panel-by-panel application with heat-forming around curves, mirrors and door handles. Edges are tucked or trimmed cleanly rather than left visible, and every panel gets a quality-control check before we move to the next.

05

Reveal

A final full-car inspection in good light, then it's yours. We'll walk you through care in the first 48 hours — wash restrictions, curing time — so the wrap lasts as long as it should.
